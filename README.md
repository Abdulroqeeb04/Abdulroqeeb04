## Featured Projects

### Maji Ndogo Water Services SQL Analysis

A SQL-based analysis of the **Maji Ndogo water services database** focused on understanding water access, queue patterns, water quality and inconsistencies within pollution records.

#### The Problem

The dataset contained information about water sources, visits, queue times, water quality and pollution records. The objective was to explore the data, identify operational patterns and investigate inconsistencies that could affect the reliability of the information.

#### Approach

- Explored and analysed multiple related database tables using SQL
- Investigated different water-source types and their usage
- Analysed queue times and repeated visits
- Reviewed water-quality records for unusual patterns
- Investigated inconsistencies in pollution classifications
- Cleaned incorrect pollution descriptions
- Used SQL queries to validate changes after cleaning

#### Key Findings

- Identified **38 pollution descriptions** that incorrectly contained `Clean`
- Found wells classified as clean despite biological contamination above the expected threshold
- Investigated **218 suspicious repeat-visit water-quality records** with a score of 10
- Identified long queue times frequently associated with shared taps
- Improved the consistency of pollution records through SQL-based data cleaning

`SQL` `MySQL` `MySQL Workbench` `Git` `GitHub`
